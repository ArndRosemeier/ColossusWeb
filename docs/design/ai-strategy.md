# Strategic AI planning

The strategic seam is `ai/splitPlanning.ts:planOptionalSplit`. `simpleAi.pickSplit` preserves mandatory opening4:4 and later height8 splits, then asks this evaluator for each optional seven. HOLD is a genuine alternative. Unique two-creature multisets exclude Titan, with the existing composition-preserving choice visited first. Every accepted candidate has a safe separation for all six engine movement rolls. Parent and child are both present in projected occupancy; no destination overlap or double teleport is allowed. An unfinished screen, losing attack, or overwhelming incoming attack on either piece cannot be paid for with recruit rewards. The two pieces share caretaker supply through `jointSplitGrowth`; only combat improvement or an available higher recruiting line counts as growth.

The evaluator balances attainable growth, retained development and the investment moved to a small child. This is a conservative bounded heuristic, not a game solver. Default optimization inspects four ranked move endpoints per step plus staying where legal, up to1024 joint final-position checks; at most21 two-creature multisets exist at height7. `SplitPlanningBudget` validates positive integer limits (plans<=8192, destinations<=12) and diagnostics report candidates/plans/exhaustion/movement queries. Truncation can conservatively HOLD or retain a previously fully screened plan, never bless an untested candidate. These are the future difficulty work limits; this slice adds no saved difficulty setting. Candidate safety uses approximate combat buckets and next enemy-roll reach, rather than simulating future battles, several turns, or enemy policy. `safeJointRoute` is now used both before splitting and during actual Move. `splitMovePolicy` recognizes siblings through the engine's existing moveOriginHex (splitParentId is cleared at beginMovePhase), recomputes against the actual roll/occupancy, fixes already-moved pieces and protects safe unmoved HOLDs from later generic/RNG movement through evaluateMove's held-legion filter. No route or new engine field is persisted. The search enumerates both real move orders and recomputes engine listAllMoves after the first command, preserving occupancy and the consumed one-teleport limit. An independent review found and closed the former execution gap where approved routes were discarded.

If a mandatory opening or changed/adopted position lacks a certified safe continuation, the API explicitly reports `status: 'least-loss'` and selects the lowest estimated loss among its bounded two-move routes and every legal single leave; Titan-loss cost dominates growth. This diagnostic is not a fake safety guarantee or a new UI/command. Already-separated safe pieces may HOLD; a group with a required legal walk receives a legal leave rather than an illegal Done. The emergency fallback does not claim exhaustive multi-step global optimality. Ordinary moves for unrelated legions remain the existing policy; this is recomputed pair survival, not a persisted global multi-legion plan.

`boardSpatial.candidateReachCounts` derives enemy reach with actual candidate occupancy (including a vacated source). Its decision-local bounded512-entry FIFO cache keys occupancy, player scores and lord/Titan teleport capabilities. Eviction changes cost only. Reach uses the engine's six-roll previews; it does not recreate gate rules. Original reachCounts remains a baseline view for diagnostics. No threat outcome is cached across caretaker changes. Variant geometry is fixed for a decision.

`splitChoice` no longer calls a terminal creature surplus merely because it has no upgrade edges. Forced split composition still protects lords and useful recruiting counts; opening composition enumerates legal basic triples, preferring completed pairs, shared development and balanced combat value, with personality lord preference and seeded tie variation retained.

`supportPlanning.planReinforcement` carries strongest available immediate reinforcement selection for estimates, actual turn4 and actual post-battle reinforcement. `planSummon` shares source ranking with the estimator and real command: evaluate the first eligible creature the engine actually transfers, subtract25% donor opportunity cost, and preserve a Titan's sole escort. Actual windows/source eligibility remain engine-owned; used/closed summoning and already-used reinforcement do not add phantom support. Personalities keep their movement/combat/growth preferences; random support refusal and the unused splitChance setting are removed.

`engine/recruit.listAvailableDevelopmentEdges` filters the cached immutable variant graph against current target supply on every call. Composition progress also excludes a line whose missing recruiters exceed remaining stock; already-complete groups retain their available upgrade. The development heuristic changes, while legal recruits/commands and the movement-preview UI ranking remain untouched. Because Enter shares this strategic scorer, it also stops preferring exhausted future paths.
